*Promises* is a collaborative album between British electronic producer Floating Points (Sam Shepherd), the legendary saxophonist Pharoah Sanders, and the London Symphony Orchestra. The result is something that defies easy categorization.

## The Structure

The album is built around a single seven-note motif that repeats, shifts, and evolves across nine movements. What begins as a minimalist harpsichord figure gradually opens up into sweeping orchestral passages and Sanders' unmistakable tenor saxophone — warm, searching, spiritual.

It's less an album and more a single continuous piece. The 46-minute runtime feels both epic and intimate, demanding your full attention yet never abrasive.

## Why It Stays With Me

There's something deeply meditative about *Promises*. Every time I listen, I notice a new detail — a subtle synth layer, a shift in the string arrangement, a breath between saxophone phrases. It rewards patience in an age that rarely asks for it.

It was also one of Pharoah Sanders' final recordings before his passing in 2022, which gives every note an added poignancy.

> Put on headphones, close your eyes, and let it wash over you.
