Aftersun

A quietly devastating portrait of memory, fatherhood, and the things we can't understand until it's too late.

movie Director Charlotte Wells · calendar_today Release 2022 · group Cast Paul Mescal, Frankie Corio · schedule Runtime 102 min

Charlotte Wells’ debut feature is a masterclass in restraint. Set mostly during a father-daughter holiday at a Turkish resort in the late 1990s, the film unfolds as a series of fragmented memories.

The Power of What’s Unsaid

Paul Mescal delivers a career-defining performance as Calum, a young father hiding profound sadness behind a gentle smile. But the film truly belongs to Frankie Corio as Sophie, whose 11-year-old gaze captures everything and nothing at once.

What makes Aftersun so devastating is how it trusts its audience. Wells never explains Calum’s pain — we only see it through Sophie’s incomplete understanding, replayed decades later through adult eyes searching for clues she missed.

Standout Moments

  • The “Under Pressure” sequence — one of the most emotionally overwhelming scenes I’ve ever experienced
  • The recurring motif of the strobe-lit dance floor, Sophie reaching for a father she can never quite reach
  • The final pan, which I won’t spoil, but it broke me completely

A film that doesn’t just reward rewatches — it deepens with them.
