The Midnight Library

A beautifully woven tale about second chances and the infinite possibilities of life.

star Rating 8.5/10.0 · edit Author Matt Haig · calendar_today Published true · business Publisher Viking · description Pages 304

Between life and death there is a library, and within that library, the shelves go on forever. Every book provides a chance to try another life you could have lived.

Why I Loved It

Matt Haig manages to wrap profound philosophical questions in a warm, accessible story. The idea of the Midnight Library — a place where you can sample all the lives you might have lived — is both comforting and haunting.

The writing is simple but effective, and Nora Seed is a protagonist many of us can relate to: someone who feels like they’ve disappointed everyone, including themselves.

Key Takeaways

  • Regret is a trap. Every choice closes some doors and opens others.
  • The “perfect” life doesn’t exist — every path has its own struggles.
  • What matters is being present in the life you’re actually living.

“The only way to learn is to live.”
